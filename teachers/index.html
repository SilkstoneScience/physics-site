For teachers

Tools for IB DP Physics teachers, built from the notes, questions and challenges on this site. Choose a subtopic and the parts you want, then download the result.

Work in progress. Both tools are still in development. Spotted a problem or have a suggestion? Let me know.

Lesson slides

Make a PowerPoint deck for a subtopic: tick the curriculum points and kinds of slide you want, and see the estimated teaching time before you download. Decks have aims, a concept-challenge hook, clear bullet points, key equations, diagrams and worked examples whose equations you can edit in PowerPoint, plus quick checks and an optional Paper 2 question from the question bank.

Worksheets

Build a student revision sheet for a subtopic, with a hook, ToK and NoS, revision notes, worked examples and a self-test, plus matching teacher notes with the answers. Download both as PDF, for SL or HL.
