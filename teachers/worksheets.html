Revision sheet builder In development

For teachers: choose a subtopic and the parts you want, and the page builds a student revision sheet and a matching set of teacher notes with answers. Download either one as a PDF.

Work in progress. The sheets may change as the tool develops. Spotted a problem or have a suggestion? Let me know.

Parts of the sheet

The download buttons open your browser's print window: choose Save as PDF as the printer. The link saves your choices, so you (or a colleague) can rebuild exactly the same sheet.
