Lesson slide generator In development

For teachers: choose a subtopic, tick the curriculum points you want to teach and the kinds of slide you need, then download a PowerPoint deck. It has a concept-challenge hook, aims, an estimated teaching time, clear bullet points, worked examples whose equations you can edit in PowerPoint, quick checks, and any Paper 2 questions you choose from the question bank.

Work in progress. Themes A, B and C are ready; Themes D and E will follow. Spotted a problem or have a suggestion? Let me know.

Curriculum points

The points use the wording of the IB guide (shortened where it is long). · ·

Slides to include

The deck opens in PowerPoint with editable equations. Google Slides and Keynote can open it too, but they show the equations as pictures. The page address saves your choices, so you (or a colleague) can make the same deck again.
