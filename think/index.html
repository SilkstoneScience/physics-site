Think deeper

Physics is more than calculations. These pages ask you to think about why things happen, to wrestle with paradoxes that puzzled great physicists, and to ask how we know what we know.

  • Concept challenges

    Short situations that test whether you really understand the physics. Predict first, then check. Sorted by topic.

  • Big questions

    Paradoxes, contradictions and unsolved mysteries, from the dark night sky to dark matter. Some are solved; some nobody has answered yet.

  • ToK and the nature of science

    How physics produces knowledge: the aspects of NoS, knowledge questions, and how to use the boxes on each topic page.

Each challenge and big question has a Show on the board button, which shows it full screen in large text with the answer hidden, for discussing in class. Challenges you have tried are marked "✓ Done", saved only in this browser.
