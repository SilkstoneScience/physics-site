Learning and problem solving

  • Physics Online ↗ A level lessonsClear lessons and worked examples covering most of the course: good for relearning a topic from scratch.
  • Flipping Physics ↗ AP Physics lessonsConcepts explained step by step, then worked problems, especially in mechanics (A.1–A.4).
  • Michel van Biezen ↗ Worked examplesThousands of short worked examples, one problem at a time.
  • Khan Academy ↗ LessonsShort lessons on core ideas, with worked examples: search the channel for your topic.
  • The Organic Chemistry Tutor ↗ Worked examplesDespite the name, many physics videos: each works through a type of problem step by step. The channel also covers maths and chemistry, so search for your topic.
  • Crash Course Physics ↗ Lesson seriesA 46-episode series giving a quick, clear overview of each area of physics.

General interest

  • Veritasium ↗ ScienceExperiments, misconceptions and how we know what we know.
  • 3Blue1Brown ↗ MathsBeautiful visual explanations of maths and the physics that uses it.
  • SmarterEveryDay ↗ Science and engineeringExploring how things work, often with high-speed cameras.
  • Sixty Symbols ↗ Physics and astronomyPhysicists at the University of Nottingham explain the ideas behind the symbols.
  • Physics Girl ↗ PhysicsExperiments and demonstrations that explore surprising physics.
  • Steve Mould ↗ ScienceSurprising experiments, explained carefully.
  • Kurzgesagt – In a Nutshell ↗ ScienceAnimated big-picture videos on space, energy and the universe.
