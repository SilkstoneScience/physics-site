General

  • phyphox ↗ RWTH Aachen UniversityTurns your phone into a lab: its accelerometer, microphone, light sensor and more become measuring instruments. Free for Android and iPhone.
  • Tracker ↗ Open Source PhysicsFree video analysis: film a motion, then track it frame by frame to get position–time data.
  • Desmos Graphing Calculator ↗ DesmosPlot data, add a best-fit line and test a relationship.
  • Algodoo ↗ AlgoryxA free 2D physics sandbox: build machines and see forces and energy in action.

  • Inelastic Collision ↗ phyphoxFind the energy lost each time a ball bounces.
