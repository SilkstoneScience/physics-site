How to play
  • Choose a lock to see its question. A correct answer opens it and shows a symbol and a digit.
  • A wrong answer jams the lock: that option is crossed out and 30 seconds are added to your time.
  • Stuck? A hint rules out one wrong answer, for 1 minute. The Σ Equations button shows the data-booklet equations for the question's topic, free.
  • When every lock in a room is open, key the digits into the door in the order of the symbols on it.
  • Read the explanation after each lock opens: that's where the revision happens.
  • Playing as a class? Press Show on the board to fill the screen.

The questions come from the question bank (multiple choice). Your best times are saved only in this browser.
