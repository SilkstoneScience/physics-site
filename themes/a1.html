Key equations: constant acceleration

For an object moving in a straight line with constant acceleration $a$, starting with velocity $u$ and reaching velocity $v$ after time $t$, with displacement $s$:

$$v = u + at \qquad s = ut + \tfrac{1}{2}at^{2}$$ $$v^{2} = u^{2} + 2as \qquad s = \frac{(u + v)}{2}\,t$$

Only use these when the acceleration is constant. If the acceleration changes, use the gradient of a velocity–time graph and the area under it instead.
