Animations

Short animations that play by themselves: watch an idea unfold one step at a time. Each one loops: click or tap the picture to pause it, and use Full screen for the board. Each has its own background music, made by your browser as it plays; browsers start sound only after a first click or tap, and the ♪ Music button turns it off (your choice is remembered on this device). Every animation also describes its steps in words underneath.

Physics skills

  • Powers of ten

    Zoom out from a proton to the observable universe: about 42 orders of magnitude.

Theme A: Space, time and motion

  • A.1 Motion graphs

    A car speeds up, stops and reverses while its s–t, v–t and a–t graphs draw themselves.

  • A.2 What exactly is friction?

    From a block that won't budge to the atoms at the surfaces: bumps, real contact spots, and bonds that form and break.

  • A.2 Collisions, explosions and rockets

    Momentum before and after elastic and sticky collisions and an explosion, then how a rocket moves by throwing gas backwards.

  • A.3 A bungee jump

    Energy flows between gravitational, kinetic and elastic stores, then into thermal energy, while the total stays the same.

  • A.4 The rolling race HL

    A hoop, a disc and a ball race down a slope. Which wins, and why doesn't mass matter?

  • A.5 Space–time diagrams HL

    World lines, the tilted axes of a moving frame, simultaneity and time dilation.

Theme B: The particulate nature of matter

  • B.1 Heating ice to steam

    What the molecules do as ice warms, melts, boils and becomes steam, with a heating curve drawn to scale.

  • B.3 Where gas pressure comes from

    Molecules hitting the walls make the pressure. Halve the volume, or double the temperature, and it doubles.

  • B.5 Inside a wire

    Free electrons, a slow drift, collisions that cause resistance, and why the lamp lights at once.

Theme D: Fields

  • D.3 A charge in a magnetic field

    Why a moving charge goes round in a circle, why opposite charges curve opposite ways, and when the path is a helix.
