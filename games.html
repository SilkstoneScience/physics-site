Review games

Revise by playing. Every game uses real physics from the syllabus, and each one ends by showing you what to revise next. Your progress is saved only in this browser.

Teachers: teaching notes for these games (timings, lesson plans and how to run them with groups) are on the For teachers page.

Escape room

  • Physics escape room

    Choose the topics, then open each lock with a multiple-choice question and collect the exit code. Race the clock, or play as a class on the board.

Mysteries

Read the statements, test each suspect's alibi with a calculation, and write your accusation. These take about 30–50 minutes, and they work well in groups: print the case file and argue it out.

  • The vanishing meteorite

    A meteorite disappears from a museum on a stormy night. Kinematics, thermal physics, circuits and waves. SL, about 30–50 minutes.
