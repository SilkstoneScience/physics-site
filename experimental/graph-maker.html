Graph maker

Make a graph of your own data that is good enough for your IA report: uncertainty bars, a line of best fit, and the steepest and shallowest lines for the uncertainty in the gradient.

Your data stays on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere: it is saved only in this browser. To move your work to another computer, use Save progress and open the file there.

1. Your data

Type your readings, or paste them from Excel or Google Sheets (click a cell, then paste). Write each heading in IB style, quantity symbol / unit, for example length L / m, and give each column its uncertainty: a value such as 0.001, or a percentage such as 2%.
