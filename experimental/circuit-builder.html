Circuit builder Simulation

Build a circuit from the data booklet's symbols and see what really happens: every current, p.d. and meter reading is worked out as you build. B.5 Current and circuits

The circuit builder works best on a tablet or a laptop. On a phone, turn it sideways and use two fingers to zoom.

How to use the circuit builder
  • Add a component: drag it from the toolbox onto the grid, or tap it in the toolbox and then tap the grid.
  • Draw a wire: drag from one terminal (a small circle at the end of a component) to another terminal, a junction or a wire. Or tap a terminal, tap the grid to add bends, and tap where the wire should end.
  • What you see is what you get: things that touch are joined, and a wire ending on another wire makes a junction (shown with a dot). Wires that cross without a dot are not joined. Wires find their own way around components, so they never touch one by accident.
  • Put a component into a wire: hold it over a straight piece of wire. When the wire lights up, let go: it goes in, in series. (This works for new components and for ones with nothing attached.)
  • Join components end to end: drop a component so that its terminal sits exactly on another terminal (or on a loose wire end).
  • Change a component: tap it, then use the panel beside the workspace. Tap a switch to open or close it.
  • Take a component out without deleting it: tap it, then choose Take out (leave a gap) or Take out and join the wires. It is moved to one side, ready to use again.
  • Rotate: select it, then use ⟳ (clockwise) or ⟲ (anticlockwise), or press R (clockwise) or Shift+R (anticlockwise). Double-tapping turns it clockwise.
  • Move around: drag empty space; zoom with the + and − buttons, two fingers, or Ctrl and the mouse wheel. Fit shows the whole circuit.
  • Delete: select it and press Delete or the Delete button. Undo (Ctrl+Z) puts it back.
  • Meters are ideal unless you untick "ideal meter". The red terminal is +. A reading above the range shows OL, as on a real multimeter: choose a higher range.
