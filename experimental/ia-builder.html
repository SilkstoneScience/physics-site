IA builder

Build your IA report one section at a time. Each section tells you what's expected, asks you some guiding questions and ends with a checklist. When you're ready, download the whole report as a Word document. The builder guides you: every word of the report is yours.

Your work stays on this device. It is saved automatically in this browser only: nothing is sent anywhere, and your teacher can't see it until you hand it in. Clearing your browser data deletes it, so use Save progress now and then to keep a copy (on your computer or your school drive). To carry on on another computer, open that copy there with Open saved file.

No names. Don't put your name (or anyone else's) anywhere in your report. Use only IB candidate codes.
