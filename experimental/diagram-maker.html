Diagram maker

Draw a clear diagram of your experimental set-up for your IA or a practical: drag equipment onto the page, arrange it, then download it as a picture for your report.

How to use it
  1. Add equipment: drag a piece from the equipment list onto the page, or tap it to put it in the middle of the page. Use the search box to find it quickly (for example "stand" or "balance").
  2. Move it: drag it. Drag on empty paper to select several things at once (on a touch screen, one finger on empty paper moves the view; pinch to zoom).
  3. Change it: drag a corner square to resize it, the round handle to turn it, and the squares at the ends of a rule, spring or bench to make it longer. The settings panel has its exact size, angle, a flip switch and any choices (for example the water level in a beaker).
  4. Join things up: choose String, Wire, Tubing, Light ray or an arrow above the page. The dots show where lines can attach. Click (or tap) where the line starts, click at each bend, and finish with a double-click or "Finish line". A wire finishes by itself when it reaches a terminal, and an arrow after two clicks. Lines attached to equipment follow it when it moves. Press Esc or choose Select to go back to moving things.
  5. Change a line: select it and drag its dots. Double-click it to add a bend, or a bend to remove it. The settings panel changes its type, adds a label to an arrow (for example W) or makes it a smooth curve.
  6. Label it: choose Label, click the thing you want to name, then click where the words should go. Type the words in the settings panel (it starts with the equipment's name), and add its precision underneath if you like, for example "±0.01 g". Tick "Numbered labels" to show numbers on the diagram with a key underneath instead.
  7. Line things up: "Snap" keeps everything on the grid, so pieces line up neatly. Turn it off for small adjustments.
  8. Finish: "Download picture (PNG)" saves a sharp picture on a white background, cropped to your drawing, ready to put in your report. "Save progress" saves a file you can open again with "Open saved file", to change the diagram later.
  9. Keyboard: arrow keys nudge (with Shift for bigger steps), Delete removes, Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes, Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V copy and paste, Ctrl+D duplicates.

Your diagram is saved automatically, but only in this browser on this device: nothing is sent anywhere. To keep a copy, or to carry on with it on another computer, press "Save progress" and open the file later with "Open saved file".

On a phone the page is small: pinch with two fingers to zoom in. A laptop or tablet is easier.
